## Update: about/Meet Our Team is a measurement bug, not a CSS bug

**Loaded both pages in Chrome.** Inspected the section:
- WP container: `display:flex, gap:20px, bg:rgb(21,25,29), padding:100px 10px 50px`
- Hugo container: `display:block, gap:normal, bg:rgb(21,25,29), padding:100px 10px 50px` (same bg/padding, different display/gap — but section only has one child, so flex vs block doesn't visibly matter)
- Both H2: color rgb(249,250,251), weight 800, Montserrat, text-align center
- Hugo H2 fontSize 40px vs WP 36.48px (small)
- Inner-blocks-wrap: identical flex/column/center on both

**Read the actual chunk PNGs** (`tests/chunks/about/{wp,hugo}-desktop-section-7.png`):
- Both 1920×~200, dark bg, "Meet Our Team" centered, white text — visually they look IDENTICAL.
- Pixel sample at row 100: both `[21,25,29,255]` — bg pixels match exactly.
- Yet `diffPercent` reports 98.45%, `overlapDiffPercent` 98.44%.

**This looks like a pixelmatch run on misaligned images.** WP=201px tall, Hugo=199px tall. The cropData function in `diffScreenshots` crops both to min(width)×min(height) = 1920×199. But if WP has 1px of top padding extra and the crop cuts WP from y=0..198 vs Hugo y=0..198, the text bands wouldn't align vertically and antialiased text edges would differ everywhere.

Actually wait — the bg sample at y=100 was identical bg (21,25,29) in both. So most of the image IS background. The text occupies maybe 50px tall. If text is offset by 2-3 px vertically between the two images, that's ~5% of pixels disagree at most, not 98%.

**More likely cause:** the chunk screenshot for one of the two captures the FULL inside (text visible) and the other captures *something else entirely* — maybe Hugo's `.uagb-block-7fd0a09e` selector picked up a different element. But I read the PNGs and visually they look the same.

**This is now a measurement-tooling investigation, not a CSS fix.** Picking the wrong battle. Switching target.
