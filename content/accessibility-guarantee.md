# Our Accessibility guarantee for Digital Maps

We guarantee full Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA compliance for Audiom digital maps that have not been customized out of compliance. The WCAG are the standards required by [ADA Title II (Opens a new tab)](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/), [Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (Opens a new tab)](https://www.section508.gov/develop/applicability-conformance/), and [EN 301 549 (Opens a new tab)](https://accessible-eu-centre.ec.europa.eu/content-corner/digital-library/en-3015492021-accessibility-requirements-ict-products-and-services_en) (in Europe). Since some of the areas of WCAG are subjective, here is how we guarantee accessibility:

1.  [Audiom is independently audited for WCAG 2.1 AA and shows full compliance](/acr).
2.  [Audiom is built and tested by certified accessibility specialists and by real screen reader and keyboard-only users](/accessibility-statement).
3.  We remediate barriers you or your users identify.
4.  We’ll stand behind it contractually, including accessibility indemnification in our contract.
