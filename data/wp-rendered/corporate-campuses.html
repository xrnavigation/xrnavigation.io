The Challenge

Modern corporate campuses resemble mini-cities, complete with multiple buildings, recreational areas, meeting zones, and more. For employees with disabilities, especially blind and low vision employees, navigating through these vast areas can pose significant challenges. Traditional maps, that you may find on a kiosk, in a pamphlet at the front desk, on an app, or on the company’s website, that are focused solely on visual representation, fall short of providing an inclusive experience and are out of compliance with many accessibility laws.

The Solution

Audiom redefines mapping with its Web Content Accessibility Guidelines AAA compliant cross-sensory design tailored for visual, auditory, and tactile users. Offering real-time updates for dynamic corporate campuses, it integrates seamlessly into existing apps, emphasizing user-centric feedback and co-design with people with disabilities.
